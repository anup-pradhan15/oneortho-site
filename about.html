About the surgeon

Anup Pradhan, MD

Board-certified orthopedic surgeon. Vanderbilt MD, Duke fellowship. 7× D Magazine Best Doctor (2020–2026). In Dallas since 2012.

Anup Pradhan, MD · North Dallas

I trained at Vanderbilt and Duke, then practiced in Dallas — including six years as Chair of Orthopedics at Medical City Dallas. I run a small independent practice on purpose: orthopedic decisions are personal, and they deserve a surgeon who still has the conversation — not a workflow that processes you.

My focus is the boundary between preservation and replacement — knee, hip, and shoulder — including second opinions before surgery.

“You always have options. My job is to help you find yours — and to deliver world-class outcomes when surgery is the answer.”

— Anup Pradhan, MD
