Insurance verification is available before your visit. Medicare is accepted where eligible; for commercial plans, contact the office to verify your coverage before your visit. Surgery, if recommended, is planned separately.

Can this be remote?

Yes when imaging can be shared ahead. We’ll say if in-person is better.

How soon?

Most within a week once records arrive.
